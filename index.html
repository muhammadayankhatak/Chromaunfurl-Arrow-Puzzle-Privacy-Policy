01

THE BASICS

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Chromaunfurl Arrow Puzzle (“Game”), provided by BRIXEL GAMES handles information when you play the Game or visit this privacy-policy page.

We want this policy to be understandable. Below, we describe what is stored on your device, what advertising providers may collect, how information is used, and how to contact us.

02

WHAT WE KNOW

Information we handle

✧
Game progress and settings

The Game stores information such as completed levels, progress, coins, available boosters, and settings locally on your device. This version does not require a player account to play.

Advertising and device information. If the Game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), the SDK collects and shares certain data with Google, including IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions (such as taps and ad views), diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers. These practices can depend on SDK settings and applicable consent choices.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message so that we can answer your request.

03

THE WHY

How information is used

  • ✓
    Remember your progress. Keep your unlocked puzzles, scores, booster availability, and preferences on your device.
  • ✓
    Display and measure advertisements. AdMob may use relevant device and interaction information for delivering ads, measuring performance, and preventing abuse.
  • ✓
    Keep advertising services reliable. Google may use diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals as described in its policies.
  • ✓
    Respond to support questions. Use the content of messages you send us to help resolve issues.
04

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Advertising & partners

The Game may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional advertisements that may grant in-game benefits, such as coins or boosters, after completion.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information directly with Google for advertising, reporting, analytics related to ads, and fraud prevention. Google controls its own processing under its applicable policies. We do not receive your full advertising profile from Google.

This policy does not cover independent third-party websites or services you choose to visit through advertisements.

05

YOU'RE IN CONTROL

Your privacy choices

↶Clear local data

You can remove locally saved progress by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game, subject to your device's backup settings.

◎Ad preferences

Review your Android privacy/advertising settings and any consent controls the Game provides to manage applicable advertising choices.

To ask us about information you shared directly with us, including support emails, contact us using the address below. Information handled by Google is subject to Google's own controls and policies.

06

YOUNGER PLAYERS

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information to us. If you believe a child has shared personal information directly with us, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

Advertising configuration and any age-specific consent requirements should be set according to the actual audience and applicable law.

07

KEEPING THINGS SAFE

Security & retention

Locally saved gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to any device backup or restoration features. We do not operate an account database for this version of the Game.

Google's advertising SDK uses transport encryption when sending data, and Google handles the data it receives according to its privacy and retention practices. We retain emails you send to our support contact only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and meet applicable legal obligations.

This website uses no analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Because it is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses, for security and site delivery under its own policies.

08

STAYING CURRENT

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version applies. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
